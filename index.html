Got questions?

Do I need money to start?
Nope. Scout helps you find ideas that start near zero: borrow gear, pre-sell, or do a few jobs first and buy equipment from what you earn.
Do my parents need to know?
If you are under 16, yes: a parent or guardian gets a heads-up before anything kicks off. It is still 100% your business, they are just in the loop.
Is this actually free?
Completely. No account, no card, no trial-that-bills-you. The chat, the trial kit and the printable plan all cost exactly zero.
What if my idea sucks?
Then you find out in one afternoon instead of blowing your savings. Scout kills weak ideas with you, shows why, and hunts for a better one. Failure is data.
How do I get the PDF business plan?
Chat with Scout, run your paid trial week, then come back and report your numbers. Pitch your idea back, and the "Get my plan" button prints your one-page Hustler Business Plan.
